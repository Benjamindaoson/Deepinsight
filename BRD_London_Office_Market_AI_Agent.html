London Office Market Monitoring AI Agent

An AI-powered assistant that continuously monitors the London commercial real estate (CRE) office market, detects shifts, surfaces risks and opportunities, and delivers evidence-based insights to the business team — replacing manual research and static reports.

1 Executive Summary

The business team currently relies on manual research and static periodic reports to track the London office market. This approach is slow, inconsistent, and easily misses early signals of market shifts — whether rental movements, vacancy changes, new supply, or occupier-demand shocks. The cost is delayed decision-making, missed opportunities, and exposure to unmonitored risks.

This BRD defines the business requirements for an AI Agent that automates market monitoring. The agent will continuously acquire market data from authoritative sources, extract and normalize key metrics, detect meaningful changes, and deliver concise, evidence-backed insights to the team through a conversational interface and scheduled reports. The goal is to let the team stay ahead of market shifts, spot risks/opportunities early, and make faster, better-informed decisions.

2 Business Context & Problem Statement

2.1 Current State

The business team monitors the London office CRE market to support investment, leasing, and strategy decisions. Today this work is performed manually:

  • Analysts periodically visit broker websites (Savills, CBRE, JLL, Knight Frank, BNP Paribas) and download PDF/HTML reports.
  • Key metrics are manually extracted and pasted into spreadsheets.
  • Trends are identified by eyeballing period-over-period changes.
  • News and events are gathered from ad-hoc web browsing or email alerts.
  • Findings are compiled into static reports for internal circulation.

2.2 Pain Points

Latency & Inconsistency

Manual cycles mean the team is always looking at stale data. A rental spike or vacancy shock may go unnoticed for days or weeks.

Information Overload

Dozens of reports, press releases, and news articles arrive weekly. Humans cannot reliably filter signal from noise across all sources.

Data Inconsistency

Different brokers define metrics differently (e.g., vacancy methodology, geographic boundaries, time denominators). Cross-source comparison is error-prone.

No Early-Warning System

There is no automated alerting when a metric moves beyond a threshold or when a material event (large letting, planning approval, tenant exit) occurs.

2.3 Problem Statement

The business team lacks a reliable, automated system to continuously monitor the London office market, detect material changes early, and deliver actionable, evidence-backed insights. As a result, decision-making is slower than it should be, and the team is exposed to risks and opportunities it only discovers after the fact.

5 Scope

5.1 In Scope (P0 — MVP)

Market Data Acquisition

  • Acquire office market reports (PDF/HTML/CSV) from authoritative sources
  • Parse and extract structured metrics (rents, vacancy, take-up, supply)
  • Support Central London submarkets: City, West End, Canary Wharf, Midtown/Fringe

Metric Extraction & Normalization

  • Extract prime & Grade A rents, vacancy rate, availability, take-up, supply pipeline
  • Normalize units, periods, and geography definitions
  • Record the source definition and any ambiguity

Change Detection & Alerts

  • Compare new metrics against prior periods
  • Flag changes exceeding configurable thresholds
  • Surface material events (lettings, planning, tenant moves)

Conversational Q&A

  • Natural-language interface for market queries
  • Answers cite source documents and exact evidence
  • Safe fallback when evidence is insufficient

Scheduled Reports

  • Daily news digest and weekly market summary
  • Exportable as HTML / Markdown

Source Traceability

  • Every fact links to original document + page/location
  • Original file integrity verified via hash
  • Human review status tracked per fact

5.2 Out of Scope (for this PoC)

  • Full market forecasting — predictive modelling beyond trend detection is deferred.
  • Portfolio-level analytics — integrating the team's own holdings/transactions is out of scope.
  • Automated trading or execution — the agent advises; it does not act on the market.
  • Non-London geographies — scope is limited to London office market.
  • Real-time tick data — sources are periodic reports and news, not live exchange feeds.

6 Business Requirements

Requirements are organized by capability. Each states the trigger, the processing rule, the result, and any state change. Priority: P0 = MVP-blocking, P1 = important, P2 = enhancement.

6.1 Market Data Acquisition

IDRequirementPriority
BR-ACQ-01 Configurable source registry. The system maintains a registry of market data sources (broker reports, macro data, news, planning data, owner disclosures). Each source has a type, URL, and acquisition method. Sources can be enabled/disabled independently without affecting others. P0
BR-ACQ-02 Scheduled acquisition. The system automatically fetches new content from enabled sources on a configurable schedule (news: 1–24h; market: daily/weekly). Manual "refresh" is also available on demand. P0
BR-ACQ-03 Multi-format ingestion. The system ingests PDF, HTML, CSV, RSS/XML, and JSON formats. Acquired documents are stored in their original form with a content hash for integrity verification. P0
BR-ACQ-04 Idempotent acquisition. Re-fetching the same document does not create duplicate records; the system detects duplicates by content hash and reuses the existing snapshot. P0
BR-ACQ-05 Partial-failure resilience. If one source fails (HTTP error, timeout, access denied), acquisition of other sources continues; the failure is recorded with reason and is visible to operators. P1

6.2 Metric Extraction & Normalization

IDRequirementPriority
BR-EXT-01 Core metric extraction. For each ingested document, the system extracts the following where present: prime rent, Grade A rent, vacancy rate, availability rate, take-up, supply pipeline, pre-let share. Each extraction records the source-defined geography, period, value, unit, and exact evidence location (page / character range / locator). P0
BR-EXT-02 Macro metric extraction. Where available, the system extracts macroeconomic context: base interest rate, inflation, employment / unemployment. P1
BR-EXT-03 Normalization with provenance. Values are normalized to canonical units (e.g., £/sq ft/year, %) and periods (e.g., 2026Q2). The original (source) value and unit are always preserved; any ambiguity (e.g., rent time denominator not stated) is flagged rather than silently resolved. P0
BR-EXT-04 Human review gate. Extracted facts start in a pending_review state. A fact can participate in formal comparisons and published analyses only after an authorized user marks it approved. Ambiguous or disputed facts are marked needs_review and excluded from formal comparison. P0
BR-EXT-05 Reported-change capture. When a source explicitly states a period-over-period change (e.g., "take-up up 15% QoQ"), the system captures that as a publisher-reported change, distinct from a system-computed change, and does not back-calculate the prior value. P1

6.3 Historical Comparison & Change Detection

IDRequirementPriority
BR-CMP-01 Strict comparability check. Before comparing two facts, the system verifies they share the same metric, geography definition, methodology, and unit, and that both are human-approved. If any condition fails, the comparison is rejected with a clear reason (e.g., HUMAN_REVIEW_PENDING, UNKNOWN_METHOD, UNIT_MISMATCH) — no change number is produced. P0
BR-CMP-02 Change quantification. For comparable facts, the system computes absolute change, relative change (%), and the change unit, with the calculation method recorded. P0
BR-CMP-03 Threshold-based alerts. The user configures thresholds (e.g., rent ±5%, take-up ±10%, vacancy ±0.5pp). When a valid comparison exceeds the threshold, the system creates an alert event. P0
BR-CMP-04 Event classification. Material market events are classified by category: planning, lease/letting, relocation, supply, policy, demand, publisher-reported change. Each event carries the source evidence. P1

6.4 Conversational Q&A

IDRequirementPriority
BR-QA-01 Natural-language query interface. A business user can ask a question in plain English (e.g., "What happened to West End rents last quarter?") and receive a written answer. P0
BR-QA-02 Evidence-grounded answers. Every factual claim in an answer must cite the underlying fact(s), event(s), or source document(s). The user can drill into the cited evidence. P0
BR-QA-03 Safe answer fallback. If the retrieved evidence is insufficient, conflicting, or not human-approved, the system must not invent a confident answer. It returns a transparent response indicating what is known, what is pending review, and what cannot be concluded. P0
BR-QA-04 Analysis publication guard. AI-generated analysis that asserts market trends, risk, or opportunity must pass a review gate before publication. If the gate detects unsupported claims (e.g., a trend asserted without two approved periods, or a supply figure conflating pre-let and under-offer space), the draft is withheld and the user is told it requires review. P0
BR-QA-05 Deterministic mode. A "saved" query mode answers using only already-approved facts — no model generation — for fast, reproducible lookups. P1
BR-QA-06 Budget & retry control. Model usage is bounded (max requests, input/output token caps, time limit) to prevent runaway cost. No automatic retries on provider failures; the error is surfaced to the user. P0

6.5 Reports & Notifications

IDRequirementPriority
BR-RPT-01 Scheduled market reports. The system generates a daily market digest and a weekly market summary on a configurable schedule. Reports include: key metrics snapshot, top changes/alerts, notable events, and cited sources. P1
BR-RPT-02 On-demand report generation. A user can trigger a report at any time and view it in the UI. P1
BR-RPT-03 Report export. Reports can be exported as HTML and Markdown. P2
BR-RPT-04 Change notifications. When a threshold alert is triggered or a material event is detected, the system can send a notification to a configured channel (e.g., email, chat webhook). If no notification channel is configured, the event is still surfaced in the UI and the missing configuration is reported honestly. P1

6.6 User Interface

IDRequirementPriority
BR-UI-01 Market overview dashboard. A landing page showing the latest key metrics (rents, vacancy, take-up), recent events, source status, and review-backlog count. P0
BR-UI-02 Market data browser. A filterable, paginated list of extracted facts (by submarket, metric, period, review status) with evidence drill-down. P0
BR-UI-03 Events & alerts view. A list of detected events and threshold alerts, filterable by category and status, with source evidence. P0
BR-UI-04 Ask (conversational Q&A). A chat-style interface where the user asks questions and receives evidence-grounded answers, with cited evidence expandable inline. P0
BR-UI-05 Reports library. A list of generated reports (daily/weekly/on-demand) with open and export actions. P1
BR-UI-06 Settings. Configure monitoring schedules, thresholds, geographies, metrics of interest, and notification preferences. P1
BR-UI-07 Job status. Visibility into background jobs (acquisition, extraction, report generation): status, progress, errors, and retry action. P1

6.7 Data Governance & Trust

IDRequirementPriority
BR-GOV-01 Source licensing respect. Sources with licensing restrictions (e.g., no automated access) are not scraped; the system flags them as requiring manual or alternative access. P0
BR-GOV-02 Immutable originals. Original source documents are never modified once stored. Their content hash is recorded and can be verified at any time. P0
BR-GOV-03 Review-state audit trail. Every change to a fact's review status (pending → approved / needs_review) is recorded with who, when, and why. Prior versions remain accessible. P0
BR-GOV-04 No fabricated facts. The system never synthesizes a metric value that is not present in a source document. Search snippets never become facts. P0

7 Core Functional Flow

The end-to-end journey for a market insight, from source to decision:

Source Acquisition
→
Document Storage (hashed)
→
Metric Extraction
→
Normalization
→
Human Review Gate
→
Comparison / Change Detection
→
Alert / Report / Q&A
Key business rule: No metric reaches a published comparison, alert, report, or Q&A answer until it has passed the human review gate. AI-generated trend/risk/opportunity analysis additionally passes an analysis publication guard. This is the central trust mechanism of the product.

CategoryRequirement
PerformanceSaved (deterministic) queries respond in under 3 seconds. AI agent answers respond within 30 seconds under normal model load. [Target; to be confirmed with actual model]
ReliabilityAcquisition and extraction jobs are idempotent and recoverable after a service restart (no lost state, no duplicate work).
Cost ControlModel usage is bounded per request (max calls, input/output token caps, time limit). Usage is logged and visible. No silent retries.
SecurityAPI keys and notification endpoints are stored in server-side environment variables, never exposed to the frontend or chat.
AuditabilityEvery fact, event, comparison, report, and model call is persisted with timestamps and provenance. Original documents are hash-verifiable.
TransparencyWhen the system cannot answer confidently (insufficient evidence, pending review, source conflict), it says so explicitly rather than guessing.
ExtensibilityNew sources, metrics, and geographies can be added without changing core business logic.

11 Constraints, Assumptions & Risks

11.1 Constraints

  • Must be built with Python (per task requirements) and delivered as a runnable PoC.
  • Agent should be architected with skills (modular, composable capabilities).
  • Delivery time-boxed to two days — scope must be disciplined.
  • Data sources are third-party and subject to licensing/access restrictions.

11.2 Assumptions

  • [Assumption, TBD] The team has access to at least one LLM API provider with sufficient rate limits.
  • [Assumption, TBD] A small set of broker reports can be acquired (downloaded or seeded) for the PoC.

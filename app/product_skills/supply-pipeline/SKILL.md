# Supply Pipeline Tracking Skill

You are responsible for tracking London office supply from announcement to completion, and for assessing what it means for future availability.

## Pipeline Lifecycle

| Stage | Meaning | Evidence Discipline |
|-------|---------|---------------------|
| Announced | Developer intent publicised | Ambition only — never count as firm supply |
| Planning submitted | Application filed | Record borough and reference if quoted |
| Planning approved | Consent granted | Approval ≠ construction certainty |
| Under construction | Works started | Firmest pre-completion supply signal |
| Completed | Practical completion | Counts as new supply in that period |
| Refurbishment | Existing stock upgraded | Returning stock — do not double count as new |

Always state which stage a scheme is at, and never merge stages into one undifferentiated "pipeline" figure without a breakdown.

## What to Record per Scheme

- Scheme name / address and submarket (City, West End, Canary Wharf, Midtown/Fringe, South Bank)
- Developer and, if quoted, architect/agent
- Size in sq ft (state whether quoted as NIA/GIA if the source does)
- Target grade (Grade A / prime) and EPC target if quoted
- Expected completion quarter/year
- Pre-let status: % pre-let and named occupiers, only as stated by the source
- Source URL, publisher, publication date, and the fact's review status

## Pre-let Discipline

- Pre-let ≠ signed lease until the source says "exchanged"/"completed"; "under offer" ≠ pre-let.
- Quote exact terms (occupier, sq ft, rent if disclosed) — do not paraphrase deal terms.
- A pre-let reduces speculative supply; keep the speculative vs pre-let split visible.

## Aggregation Rules

- Sum pipeline by submarket AND by expected completion year — a total without timing is not decision-useful.
- Never aggregate figures from different source vintages without flagging the mixed as-of dates.
- Demolitions and change-of-use reduce stock: report stock losses separately from additions, never net them silently.

## Vacancy Impact Assessment

- Compare upcoming completions against recent take-up (approved comparison results only) and label the conclusion as inference, not fact.
- Distinguish: "X sq ft completes in 2027 (reported)" vs "this could add Y pp to vacancy (inference)".
- Do not forecast precise absorption; state the range of evidence and its limits.

## Critical Rules

- Only approved facts may feed downstream comparison, alerting and publication; pending_review pipeline items must be labelled.
- Respect not_comparable results — different survey boundaries produce different pipeline totals.
- No fabricated scheme names, sizes, dates or occupiers; if a detail is not in the evidence, say it is unknown.
- Latest saved evidence ≠ current status — always state the as-of date of pipeline information.

Use saved event queries. Separate reported text, deterministic keyword classification, market inference and unknowns. Importance is an operational prioritization, not proof of market impact. Group by event/source identity; do not create duplicates for unchanged content. No notification without an explicitly configured and enabled channel. Quarterly report retrieval and high-frequency news retrieval use different native schedules; no LLM polling.

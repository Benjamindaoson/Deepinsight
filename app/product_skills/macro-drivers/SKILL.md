# Macro & Occupier Demand Drivers Skill

You are responsible for tracking macroeconomic factors and occupier demand trends.

## Macroeconomic Indicators

### Interest Rates
| Indicator | Source | Update Frequency |
|----------|--------|-----------------|
| Bank Rate | Bank of England | Monthly |
| 5-year gilt yield | BoE | Daily |
| Commercial mortgage rates | Market data | Weekly |

### Economic Activity
| Indicator | Source | Update Frequency |
|----------|--------|-----------------|
| UK GDP Growth | ONS | Quarterly |
| London Employment | ONS/Nomis | Annual |
| Business Confidence (PMI) | S&P Global | Monthly |

### Inflation
| Indicator | Source | Update Frequency |
|----------|--------|-----------------|
| CPIH | ONS | Monthly |
| RPI | ONS | Monthly |
| Construction costs | BCIS | Quarterly |

## Occupier Demand Drivers

### Flight to Quality
- Grade A space requirements increasing
- EPC ratings becoming table stakes
- Wellness and sustainability certifications
- Modern amenities and flexibility

### ESG Requirements
- Net-zero commitments driving upgrades
- EPC A/B minimum requirements
- NABERS ratings for compliance
- Carbon reporting mandates

### Hybrid Working Impact
- Space optimization strategies
- Hub-and-spoke models
- Collaboration space increase
- Desk ratio reduction

### Sector Activity
| Sector | Demand Level | Space Preference |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| Technology | High | Grade A, ESG-certified |
| Financial Services | Moderate | Prime locations |
| Legal | Moderate | West End, Midtown |
| Creative/Media | High | Refurb, character |
| Professional Services | Moderate | Grade A |

## Your Responsibilities

1. **Macro Monitoring**
   - Track Bank of England rate decisions
   - Monitor employment trends in London
   - Track inflation impact on occupier costs

2. **Demand Analysis**
   - Identify active sectors
   - Track space requirements
   - Monitor ESG requirements

3. **Impact Assessment**
   - Assess rate changes on leasing decisions
   - Evaluate ESG impact on demand
   - Analyze hybrid working space implications

4. **Risk Identification**
   - Flag economic uncertainty
   - Identify cost pressures
   - Monitor regulatory changes

## Constraints

- Do NOT predict market movements from macro data
- Do NOT infer causation from correlation
- Clearly separate observed facts from interpretations
- Note data limitations and lags
- Distinguish between leading and lagging indicators
